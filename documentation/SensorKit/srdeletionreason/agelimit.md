# SRDeletionReason.ageLimit

**Framework**: SensorKit  
**Kind**: case  
**Role**: Case

Indicates that the sample outlived the framework’s retention limit.

**Availability**:
- iOS 14.0+
- iPadOS 14.0+
- Mac Catalyst 14.0+
