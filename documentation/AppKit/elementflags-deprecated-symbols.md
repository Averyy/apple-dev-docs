# Deprecated symbols

**Framework**: AppKit  
**Role**: API Collection

Review symbols that are no longer in active development, and their recommended replacements.

## Overview

Although these symbols are available for use, Apple is no longer actively developing them, and may remove them in a future release. Apple recommends moving away from these symbols and using their indicated replacements instead. Check the individual symbol pages for the recommended replacements.

## Topics

### Deprecated constants
- [NSHourMinuteDatePickerElementFlag](./nshourminutedatepickerelementflag.md)
  Display and allow editing of the hour and minute elements of the date.
- [NSHourMinuteSecondDatePickerElementFlag](./nshourminuteseconddatepickerelementflag.md)
  Display and allow editing of the hour, minute and second elements of the date.
- [NSTimeZoneDatePickerElementFlag](./nstimezonedatepickerelementflag.md)
  Display and allow editing of the time zone.
- [NSYearMonthDatePickerElementFlag](./nsyearmonthdatepickerelementflag.md)
  Display and allow editing of the year and month elements of the date.
- [NSYearMonthDayDatePickerElementFlag](./nsyearmonthdaydatepickerelementflag.md)
  Display and allow editing of the year, month and day elements of the date.
- [NSEraDatePickerElementFlag](./nseradatepickerelementflag.md)
  Display and allow editing of the era of the date, if applicable.
