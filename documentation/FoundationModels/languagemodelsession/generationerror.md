# LanguageModelSession.GenerationError

**Framework**: Foundation Models  
**Kind**: enum  
**Role**: Enumeration

An error that may occur while generating a response.

**Availability**:
- iOS 26.0+ - Deprecated in 27.0
- iPadOS 26.0+ - Deprecated in 27.0
- Mac Catalyst 26.0+ - Deprecated in 27.0
- macOS 26.0+ - Deprecated in 27.0
- visionOS 26.0+ - Deprecated in 27.0

> **Deprecated**: When your app is built against Xcode 27 or later, the framework throws [`LanguageModelError`](../languagemodelerror.md), [`SystemLanguageModel.Error`](../systemlanguagemodel/error.md), or [`LanguageModelSession.Error`](./error.md) instead. If your app deploys to iOS 26, macOS 26 or visionOS 26, match the new types inside an availability check and fall back to [`LanguageModelSession.GenerationError`](./generationerror.md) for earlier system versions. Apps built against Xcode 26 continues to receive [`LanguageModelSession.GenerationError`](./generationerror.md).
