# Product.PromotionInfo.Visibility.hidden

**Framework**: StoreKit  
**Kind**: case

A visibility value that hides a promoted Apple In-App Purchase on the App Store on a user’s device.

**Availability**:
- iOS 16.4+
- iPadOS 16.4+
- Mac Catalyst 16.4+

## Declaration

```swift
case hidden
```

#### Discussion

A promoted Apple In-App Purchase with this visibility setting isn’t visible on the App Store on a user’s device.
