# title

**Framework**: CarPlay  
**Kind**: property

Title of card presented to configure waypoints along a route. If no title is provided, the card title will default to “Trip Overview”.

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- Mac Catalyst 27.0+ (Beta)
