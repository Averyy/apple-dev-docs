# Voice-based conversation

**Framework**: CarPlay

Build an app that responds to questions or requests and performs actions.

## Overview

A voice-based conversation app gives people a way to perform actions and get information using voice conversations. Create this type of app if you provide access to a generative model or similar services. Give people a way to initiate conversations from your app’s main template, and use additional templates to offer other services and features.

This type of app supports the following CarPlay templates:

- [`CPInformationTemplate`](./cpinformationtemplate.md)
- [`CPPointOfInterestTemplate`](./cppointofinteresttemplate.md)
- [`CPTabBarTemplate`](./cptabbartemplate.md)
- [`CPListTemplate`](./cplisttemplate.md)
- [`CPGridTemplate`](./cpgridtemplate.md)
- [`CPVoiceControlTemplate`](./cpvoicecontroltemplate.md)
- [`CPActionSheetTemplate`](./cpactionsheettemplate.md)
- [`CPAlertTemplate`](./cpalerttemplate.md)
