# Driving task

**Framework**: CarPlay

Build an app that helps people perform driving-related tasks.

## Overview

A driving-task app helps people accomplish tasks they need to do while driving. Create this type of app to display vehicle-related information, manage trip checklists, provide road-related information, or perform other tasks that don’t require frequent updates. Display your app’s main features initially using the standard templates and present other templates to display detailed information. Your app can display points of interest as long as they’re related to the driving experience and not the focus of your app.

This type of app supports the following CarPlay templates:

- [`CPInformationTemplate`](./cpinformationtemplate.md)
- [`CPPointOfInterestTemplate`](./cppointofinteresttemplate.md)
- [`CPTabBarTemplate`](./cptabbartemplate.md)
- [`CPListTemplate`](./cplisttemplate.md)
- [`CPGridTemplate`](./cpgridtemplate.md)
- [`CPVoiceControlTemplate`](./cpvoicecontroltemplate.md)
- [`CPActionSheetTemplate`](./cpactionsheettemplate.md)
- [`CPAlertTemplate`](./cpalerttemplate.md)
