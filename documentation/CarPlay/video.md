# Video

**Framework**: CarPlay

Build an app that plays video content exclusively.

## Overview

A video app plays video content through the CarPlay interface. Create this type of app if your app manages video content exclusively and doesn’t offer audio-only content. In your app’s interface, organize content using lists and other standard elements. Initiate the playing of video content using a video framework like [AVKit](../AVKit/AVKit.md) or [AVFoundation](../AVFoundation/AVFoundation.md).

This type of app supports the following CarPlay templates:

- [`CPNowPlayingTemplate`](./cpnowplayingtemplate.md)
- [`CPSearchTemplate`](./cpsearchtemplate.md)
- [`CPTabBarTemplate`](./cptabbartemplate.md)
- [`CPListTemplate`](./cplisttemplate.md)
- [`CPGridTemplate`](./cpgridtemplate.md)
- [`CPVoiceControlTemplate`](./cpvoicecontroltemplate.md)
- [`CPActionSheetTemplate`](./cpactionsheettemplate.md)
- [`CPAlertTemplate`](./cpalerttemplate.md)

> **Note**: CarPlay video apps also run on Apple HomeView without any modifications.
