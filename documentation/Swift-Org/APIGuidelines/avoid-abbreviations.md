# Avoid abbreviations

**Framework**: Swift-Org/APIGuidelines  
**Role**: Article

Avoid abbreviations.

## Overview

Abbreviations, especially non-standard ones, are effectively terms-of-art, because understanding depends on correctly translating them into their non-abbreviated forms.

> **Note**: The intended meaning for any abbreviation you use should be easily found by a web search.
