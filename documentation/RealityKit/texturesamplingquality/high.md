# TextureSamplingQuality.high

**Framework**: RealityKit  
**Kind**: case

Computes with high sampling rates, reducing texture noise in high-frequency areas.

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- tvOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- visionOS 27.0+ (Beta)
