# TextureSamplingQuality.low

**Framework**: RealityKit  
**Kind**: case

Low sampling rates can result in higher noise in high-frequency areas and banding in low-frequency gradients.

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- tvOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- visionOS 27.0+ (Beta)
