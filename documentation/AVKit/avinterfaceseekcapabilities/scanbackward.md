# scanBackward

**Framework**: AVKit  
**Kind**: property

The source supports backward scanning at accelerated rates for rewind operations. Enables rapid reverse progression through content at speeds greater than normal playback.

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- Mac Catalyst 27.0+ (Beta)
