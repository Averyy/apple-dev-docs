# requiresLinearPlayback

**Framework**: AVKit  
**Kind**: property

Indicates whether this segment must be played sequentially without seeking or skipping. Typically used for advertisements or important announcements.

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- Mac Catalyst 27.0+ (Beta)
