# Inputs

**Type:** collectionGroup

Learn about the various methods people use to control your app or game and enter data.

- [Action button](/design/human-interface-guidelines/action-button) - The Action button gives people quick access to their favorite features on supported iPhone and Apple Watch models.
- [Apple Pencil and Scribble](/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-pencil-and-scribble) - Apple Pencil helps make drawing, handwriting, and marking effortless and natural, in addition to performing well as a pointer and UI interaction tool.
- [Camera Control](/design/human-interface-guidelines/camera-control) - The Camera Control provides direct access to your app’s camera experience.
- [Digital Crown](/design/human-interface-guidelines/digital-crown) - The Digital Crown is an important hardware input for Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch.
- [Eyes](/design/human-interface-guidelines/eyes) - In visionOS, people look at a virtual object to identify it as a target they can interact with.
- [Focus and selection](/design/human-interface-guidelines/focus-and-selection) - Focus helps people visually confirm the object that their interaction targets.
- [Game controls](/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-controls) - Precise, intuitive game controls enhance gameplay and can increase a player’s immersion in the game.
- [Gestures](/design/human-interface-guidelines/gestures) - A gesture is a physical motion that a person uses to directly affect an object in an app or game on their device.
- [Gyroscope and accelerometer](/design/human-interface-guidelines/gyro-and-accelerometer) - On-device gyroscopes and accelerometers can supply data about a device’s movement in the physical world.
- [Keyboards](/design/human-interface-guidelines/keyboards) - A physical keyboard can be an essential input device for entering text, playing games, controlling apps, and more.
- [Nearby interactions](/design/human-interface-guidelines/nearby-interactions) - Nearby interactions support on-device experiences that integrate the presence of people and objects in the nearby environment.
- [Pointing devices](/design/human-interface-guidelines/pointing-devices) - People can use a pointing device like a trackpad or mouse to navigate the interface and initiate actions.
- [Remotes](/design/human-interface-guidelines/remotes) - The Siri Remote is the primary input method for Apple TV, helping people feel connected to onscreen content from across the room.
