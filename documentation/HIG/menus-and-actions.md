# Menus and actions

**Type:** collectionGroup

- [Activity views](/design/human-interface-guidelines/activity-views) - An activity view — often called a *share sheet* — presents a range of tasks that people can perform in the current context.
- [Buttons](/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons) - A button initiates an instantaneous action.
- [Context menus](/design/human-interface-guidelines/context-menus) - A context menu provides access to functionality that’s directly related to an item, without cluttering the interface.
- [Dock menus](/design/human-interface-guidelines/dock-menus) - On a Mac, people can secondary click an app’s or game’s icon in the Dock to reveal a Dock menu, which presents both system-provided and custom items.
- [Edit menus](/design/human-interface-guidelines/edit-menus) - An edit menu lets people make changes to selected content in the current view, in addition to offering related commands like Copy, Select, Translate, and Look Up.
- [Home Screen quick actions](/design/human-interface-guidelines/home-screen-quick-actions) - Home Screen quick actions give people a way to perform app-specific actions from the Home Screen.
- [Menus](/design/human-interface-guidelines/menus) - A menu reveals its options when people interact with it, making it a space-efficient way to present commands in your app or game.
- [Ornaments](/design/human-interface-guidelines/ornaments) - In visionOS, an ornament presents controls and information related to a window, without crowding or obscuring the window’s contents.
- [Pop-up buttons](/design/human-interface-guidelines/pop-up-buttons) - A pop-up button displays a menu of mutually exclusive options.
- [Pull-down buttons](/design/human-interface-guidelines/pull-down-buttons) - A pull-down button displays a menu of items or actions that directly relate to the button’s purpose.
- [The menu bar](/design/human-interface-guidelines/the-menu-bar) - On a Mac or an iPad, the menu bar at the top of the screen displays the top-level menus in your app or game.
- [Toolbars](/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars) - A toolbar provides convenient access to frequently used commands, controls, navigation, and search.
