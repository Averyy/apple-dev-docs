# Delete a Campaign

**Framework**: Apple Ads  
**Kind**: httpRequest

Deletes a specific campaign by campaign identifier.

**Availability**:
- Search Ads 5.0+

## Endpoint

`DELETE https://api.searchads.apple.com/api/v5/campaigns/{campaignId}`

## Parameters

- `campaignId` (int64) *(required)*: The unique identifier for the campaign.
