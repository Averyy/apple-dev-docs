# arrowShape(cornerPoint:)

**Framework**: PaperKit  
**Kind**: method

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+
- iPadOS 27.0+

## Declaration


## Parameters

- `cornerPoint`: The location of the arrow’s corner (where the two sides meet) in unit coordinate space.
