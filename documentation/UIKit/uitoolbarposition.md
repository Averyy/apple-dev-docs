# UIToolbarPosition

**Framework**: UIKit  
**Kind**: macro  
**Role**: Macro

Constants to identify the position of a toolbar for appearance customization.

**Availability**:
- iOS
- iPadOS
- Mac Catalyst
- tvOS
- visionOS
- watchOS

## Declaration

```c
#define UIToolbarPosition
```

#### Overview

In iOS 7 and later, these constants are defined in terms of [`UIBarPosition`](./uibarposition.md), which also introduces a new possible position: [`UIBarPosition.topAttached`](./uibarposition/topattached.md).

## Topics

### Positions
- [UIToolbarPositionAny](./uitoolbarpositionany.md)
  Indicates the toolbar may be in any position.
- [UIToolbarPositionBottom](./uitoolbarpositionbottom.md)
  Indicates the toolbar is at the top of its containing view.
- [UIToolbarPositionTop](./uitoolbarpositiontop.md)
  Indicates the toolbar is at the bottom of its containing view.
