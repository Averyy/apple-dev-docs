# Deprecated symbols

**Framework**: UIKit  
**Role**: API Collection

Review symbols that are no longer in active development, and their recommended replacements.

#### Overview

Although these symbols are available for use, Apple is no longer actively developing them, and may remove them in a future release. Apple recommends moving away from these symbols and using their indicated replacements instead. Check the individual symbol pages for the recommended replacements.

## Topics

### Deprecated constants
- [NSUnderlinePatternSolid](./nsunderlinepatternsolid.md)
  A solid line.
- [NSUnderlinePatternDot](./nsunderlinepatterndot.md)
  A line of dots.
- [NSUnderlinePatternDash](./nsunderlinepatterndash.md)
  A line of dashes.
- [NSUnderlinePatternDashDot](./nsunderlinepatterndashdot.md)
  A line of alternating dashes and dots.
- [NSUnderlinePatternDashDotDot](./nsunderlinepatterndashdotdot.md)
  A line of alternating dashes and two dots.
- [NSUnderlineByWord](./nsunderlinebyword.md)
  A line only beneath or through words, not whitespace.
