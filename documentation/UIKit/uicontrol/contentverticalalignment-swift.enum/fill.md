# UIControl.ContentVerticalAlignment.fill

**Framework**: UIKit  
**Kind**: case  
**Role**: Case

Aligns the content vertically to fill the content rectangle; images may be stretched.

**Availability**:
- iOS
- iPadOS
- Mac Catalyst
- tvOS
- visionOS
