# UIControl.ContentHorizontalAlignment.fill

**Framework**: UIKit  
**Kind**: case  
**Role**: Case

Aligns the content horizontally to fill the content rectangles; text may wrap and images may be stretched.

**Availability**:
- iOS
- iPadOS
- Mac Catalyst
- tvOS
- visionOS
