# Keynote Interchange Format release notes

**Framework**: Keynote Interchange Format  
**Role**: Article

Review the Keynote features that each version of Keynote Interchange Format supports.

**Availability**:
- Keynote Interchange Format 1.0+ (Beta)

## Overview

Keynote Interchange Format 1.0 beta is the first version of the format (`.keyx`). These Keynote features aren’t included in a version 1.0 document. Later versions of the format might support more of them.

## Presentations and themes

- Transitions and builds, including Magic Move, action builds, and motion paths, aren’t included.
- Skip Slide, the Self-Playing and Links Only presentation types, and the settings to play automatically when opened, loop, and restart if idle aren’t included.
- Soundtracks and recorded slideshows, including their narration, aren’t included.
- Dynamic backgrounds, including a theme’s dynamic background styles, aren’t included.
- Presentation passwords aren’t included.
- The presentation’s language and region aren’t included, so dates, numbers, and currency can appear differently.
- A theme’s Caption Title, Caption Text, and Presenter Notes styles, and the body text styles a slide layout sets for each indent level, aren’t included.

## Text, shapes, and objects

- Drop caps, phonetic guides, and Rotate to Horizontal in vertical text aren’t included.
- Slide numbers inserted in text aren’t included.
- Shape names, the controls that reshape a shape, and the editable points of a shape you drew or edited point by point aren’t included.
- Links on objects aren’t included.

## Images, video, and audio

- 3D objects, drawings, image galleries, web videos, live video, and live video sources aren’t included.
- Adjust Image settings aren’t included, so Reset Image can’t restore the original.
- The settings to start movies and audio on click aren’t included, so they play as soon as the slide appears.
- The button for replacing the media of a movie defined as a media placeholder isn’t included.

## Charts

- Chart styles, chart borders, and series shadows aren’t included.
- Chart backgrounds, the line styles of axes and gridlines, tick mark placement, and legend borders, backgrounds, and shadows aren’t included.
- Rounded corners and gaps on bar and column charts aren’t included.
- Trendlines, error bars, reference lines, and summary labels aren’t included.
- Pie and donut settings, such as pulled-out wedges, Rotation Angle, Inner Radius, Leader Lines, and data point names, aren’t included.
- Radar chart settings, such as Grid Shape and Rotation Angle, and series names on scatter and bubble charts aren’t included.
- Sizing bubbles by diameter and placing value labels behind the bubbles aren’t included.

## Comments and view settings

- Comments and their replies aren’t included.
- Alignment guides and view settings, such as zoom, collapsed slide groups, and whether the ruler is shown, aren’t included.
