## Topics

### Operators
- [static func + (Angle2DFloat, Angle2DFloat) -> Angle2DFloat](./plus%28_,_%29.md)
  Adds two angles and produces their sum.
- [static func += (inout Angle2DFloat, Angle2DFloat)](./pluseq%28_,_%29.md)
  Adds two angles and stores the result in the left-hand-side variable.
- [static func - (Angle2DFloat, Angle2DFloat) -> Angle2DFloat](./minus%28_,_%29.md)
  Subtracts one angle from another and produces their difference.
- [static func -= (inout Angle2DFloat, Angle2DFloat)](./minuseq%28_,_%29.md)
  Subtracts the second angle from the first and stores the difference in the left-hand-side variable.
