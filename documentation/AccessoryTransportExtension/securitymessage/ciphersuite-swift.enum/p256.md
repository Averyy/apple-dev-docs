For [`AccessoryTransport.internet`](accessorytransport/internet.md) or [`AccessoryTransport.localNetwork`](accessorytransport/localnetwork.md) support, use [`SecurityMessage.CipherSuite.xWing`](securitymessage/ciphersuite-swift.enum/xwing.md).
