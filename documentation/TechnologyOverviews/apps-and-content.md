# Apps and content

**Framework**: Technology Overviews

Build software to run on people’s devices and deliver the content and features they use in their daily lives.

Most development for [iOS](https://developer.apple.com/ios/), [iPadOS](https://developer.apple.com/ipados/), [macOS](https://developer.apple.com/macos/), [tvOS](https://developer.apple.com/tvos/), [visionOS](https://developer.apple.com/visionos/), and [watchOS](https://developer.apple.com/watchos/) starts with an app, created with frameworks in the Xcode SDK. People run apps from their devices when they want to interact with their content using the interface you provide. However, apps aren’t the only way people interact with their content. Experiences like Widgets and Live Activities help people view and interact with their content outside your app, like the Lock Screen and Home Screen. App extensions deliver your content in a lightweight package the system can run in the background instead of your app.

## Apps

[Read about apps](./apps.md)

Buid an app to deliver content, services, and experiences on Apple platforms. People download and install apps to support daily activities, for entertainment and information, and to manage their personal information. Design your app for multiple platforms, but support the features that are unique to each platform.

- Build apps using SwiftUI, UIKit, or AppKit.
- Design apps for the Apple platforms you support.
- Create apps people take with them everywhere, or use only at their desk
- Deliver content in an immersive experience.

## Games

[Read about games](./games.md)

Deliver fantastic experiences to millions of players worldwide. Apple provides the hardware, graphics, audio, social-gaming, and development and distribution systems you need to create compelling games. Deliver unparalleled immersive games in visionOS, taking virtual reality to the next level. Distribute your game on the App Store and in Apple Arcade to reach people around the world.

- Create gorgeous graphics, and get the best graphics performance from Apple’s hardware using Metal.
- Create exceptional soundtracks or immersive audio experiences, and use haptic feedback to get people’s attention.
- Add support for game controllers and other input devices.
- Support Game Center and enhance your game’s social aspects with multiplayer support, achievements, leaderboards, and challenges.

![](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.TechnologyOverviews/games@2x.png)

## System experiences

[Read about system experiences](./system-experiences.md)

Support the types of system experiences that people use regularly. Elevate your app’s most important content to the Lock Screen, Home Screen, or Dynamic Island. Give people a way to try your app’s key features, even when they don’t have the app on their device. Provide a version of your app’s interface suitable for display in a vehicle or on HomeView.

- Deliver timely and personally relevant information available at a glance.
- Display timely and relevant content using Widgets and Live Activities.
- Perform app-specific actions in Control Center, the Lock Screen, and from a device’s Action button.
- Provide an App Clip to let people try your app before they install it.
- Deliver a CarPlay version of your app’s interface.

![](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.TechnologyOverviews/experiences@2x.png)

## App extensions

[Read about app extensions](./app-extensions.md)

Extend your app’s reach into different parts of the system using *app extensions*. App extensions ship inside your app, but run as separate processes to respond to specific requests. Choose the app extensions that deliver your app’s content to the system at relevant times, and learn how to keep your app extension synchronized with your app.

- Learn about the types of app extensions you can create, including widgets, background asset downloaders, push notification services, and photo filters.
- Share data between your app and app extension.
- Let other developers extend your app by offering your own app extension programming model.
- Add low-level security and networking features to the system.
