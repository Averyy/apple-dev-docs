# Interface

**Framework**: Technology Overviews

Design and build the interface for your app and other experiences.

Apple platforms define the overall look for views and controls, and your app-builder technology determines how you create and manage your interface. You can build your interface with standard views and controls, a mixture of standard and custom views, or entirely custom content.

## Interface fundamentals

[Read about interface fundamentals](./interface-fundamentals.md)

No matter which app-builder technology you choose, most of the components you use to build your interface are the same. Before you build your interface, explore the components available to you, and learn how different platforms use those components. You can also investigate other technologies that impact the design of your interface and how you display content.

- Learn about windows, views, and other visual elements available to you.
- Manage app-related assets, and learn how to load them locally or from a remote server.
- Support common features like internationalization, accessibility, undo and redo, and the pasteboard.
- Adapt iOS apps for dynamic resizing, vertical controls, and multiple displays on iPhone Duo.

## Liquid Glass

[Read about Liquid Glass](./liquid-glass.md)

Interfaces across Apple platforms feature a dynamic material called Liquid Glass, which combines the optical properties of glass with a sense of fluidity. Learn how to leverage Liquid Glass to make sure your interface looks at home on Apple platforms.

- Embrace the visual style for materials, controls, and app icons.
- Provide a universal navigation and search experience across platforms.
- Ensure your interface’s organization and layout looks consistent with other apps and system experiences.
- Adopt best practices for windows, modals, menus, and toolbars.
- Test your app to provide a great experience across platforms.

![](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.TechnologyOverviews/liquid-glass-technology-overview-thumbnail.png)
