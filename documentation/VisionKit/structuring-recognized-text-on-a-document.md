# Structuring recognized text on a document

**Framework**: VisionKit

Detect, recognize, and structure text on a business card or receipt using Vision and VisionKit.

**Availability**:
- iOS 13.0+
- iPadOS 13.0+
- Mac Catalyst 13.0+
- Xcode 11.3+
