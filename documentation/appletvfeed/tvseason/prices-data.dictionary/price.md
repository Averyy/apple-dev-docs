# TvSeason.Prices.Price

**Framework**: Apple TV Feed  
**Kind**: dictionary

Information about a pricing offer.

**Availability**:
- AppleTVFeed 1.0+

## Declaration

```swift
object TvSeason.Prices.Price
```

## Properties

- `currencyCode` (string): The currency for this price offer.
- `price` (number): The price for this offer.
- `priceType` (string): The type of availability for the content. The types vary by line of business and entity type.
- `quality` (string): The quality for this product. The quality values vary by line of business and entity type.
