# remove(notification:)

**Framework**: Accessory Notifications  
**Kind**: method  
**Required**: Yes

Removes a previously posted notification from your accessory.

**Availability**:
- iOS 26.4+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 26.4+ (Beta)
