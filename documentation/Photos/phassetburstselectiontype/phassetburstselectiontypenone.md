# PHAssetBurstSelectionTypeNone

**Framework**: Photos  
**Kind**: case  
**Role**: Enumeration Case

The asset is not marked as a favorite member of its burst sequence or is not a member of a burst sequence.

**Availability**:
- iOS 8.0+
- iPadOS 8.0+
- Mac Catalyst 13.1+

- tvOS 10.0+
- visionOS 1.0+
