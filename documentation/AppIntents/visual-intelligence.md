# Visual intelligence

**Framework**: App Intents

Integrate your app with visual intelligence and include your content in its search results.

#### Overview

People use visual intelligence to learn about places and objects around them and onscreen. To integrate your app with visual intelligence and include your app’s content in search results, use the [`Visual Intelligence`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/VisualIntelligence) framework to receive information captured by visual intelligence and use App Intents and an [`IntentValueQuery`](intentvaluequery.md) to return matching content.
