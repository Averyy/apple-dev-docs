# Integrating your music app with Apple Intelligence

**Framework**: App Intents

Adopt the audio and clock schemas so people can play music and set alarms with Siri.

**Availability**:
- iOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- iPadOS 27.0+ (Beta)
- Mac Catalyst 27.0+ (Beta)
- Xcode 27.0+ (Beta)
